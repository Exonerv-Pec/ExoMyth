# Calendar of the Coast

How [[Rivertown]] counts time: twelve months of thirty days, then the five days of the Long Night, when the harbour closes and nobody sails. A week has six days; Marketday is the busiest.

Years are counted from the founding of the first river crossing, the start of the Age of the River (AR). The [[Siege of Rivertown]] began in the last days of Leaffall 429 and ended in Thaw 430.

Because this page says `calendar: true`, every date in the world is read with these months: `born: 3 Frostmonth 412`, `years: 28 Leaffall 429 – Thaw 430`.
