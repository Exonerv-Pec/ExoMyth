# Welcome to Exomyth

This is a demo world. Every page is a plain Markdown file, and the relations between pages live in the `relations:` block at the top of a page.

Open [[Kira Vale]] and look at the **Relations** panel on the right: it always shows its groups in the same order.

- Link to a page by writing `[[Page name]]`
- Hover a link to preview the page it points to
- Give a relation a type in the panel on the right: "Add a relation"
- Pin ⭐ important relations so they always come first in their group
- Switch to **Graph** at the top to see how the whole world is connected
- Switch to **Cards** to browse the world by folder, and open the dossier of a page: for example, the members and allies of the [[Order of the Silver River]]
- In **Cards**, the **Family tree** tab draws the family of a character from relations like `father`, `mother` and `spouse`: try [[Kira Vale]]
- Open [[Silver Coast]] for an interactive map with pins; switch on **Show by year** to move through the years
- Switch to **Timeline** to see who lived when and what happened, like the [[Siege of Rivertown]]. Press on the years scale and drag: the graph, cards and maps follow the same year
- Give a page its years in the **Years** fields of the panel on the right, or write `born:`, `died:`, `founded:` or `year:` at the top of the page
- Switch to **Book** to write the story itself: chapters and scenes of [[The Silver Thread]] on a board, read straight through or downloaded as one file
- Start a new page from a template (Character, Place, Event…) with the **+** at the top left; templates can be changed and new ones added
- Catch an idea from anywhere with **Alt+I** (or the light bulb at the top left): it lands on an Inbox page with the date
- Save the whole world as one **.zip** with the arrow button at the top left, for a backup or another computer
- Drop an image, video or audio file into the editor to add it to a page (see the map of [[Rivertown]])
- Give a page `playlist: true` and a list of audio files to get a player with its own tracks: open [[Rivertown Evening]]

Start here: [[Kira Vale]], [[Arn Vale]], [[Rivertown]], [[Order of the Silver River]].
