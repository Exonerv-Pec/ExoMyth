# The Silver Coast

The coast around [[Rivertown]], as [[Brother Tomas]] copied it from the Order's archive.

Switch on **Show by year** under the map and move the slider: the Old Watchtower stood until 405, the [[Order of the Silver River|Order]] built its fortress in 371, and Bo Tarn's smugglers found their cove in 395.
