# Arrival at Rivertown

Kira reached the river gate an hour before dawn. The bridge was already crowded with carts going the wrong way: out of the city, not in.

"They say the army is three days off," the gatekeeper told her, without looking up. "Go home, girl."

"This is home," Kira said, and walked past him.
