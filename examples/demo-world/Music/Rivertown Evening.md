# Rivertown Evening

Music for the quiet hour after the market closes in [[Rivertown]]. Open this page in **Reading** or **Side by side**: a player sits above the text, with the tracks below it to pick from.

Any page can be a playlist. Give it `playlist: true` at the top and list audio files as items, like these three tracks, which are made for this demo and live in the `attachments` folder:

- ![[Fiddle in the rain.wav]]
- ![[Last call.wav|Last call, slow]]
- ![[Dawn over the river.wav]]

A track whose file is missing is marked and skipped. Make a playlist of your own from the **+** menu: pick the Playlist template.
